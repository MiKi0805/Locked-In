## The Loop Collapse
The player survives long enough that the simulation begins to break down. Rules start changing, anomalies appear, and eventually, the system resets itself with a new set of mechanics—trapping them in a new cycle or offering a glimpse of escape.

## The System Core
By carefully navigating the world and uncovering hidden patterns, the player finds the core of the system—an entity governing the entire simulation. Destroying or manipulating it breaks the loop, leading to an unknown fate.

## The Boundary Revelation
The player expands the area too much, eventually discovering an invisible boundary that shouldn’t exist. This realization shatters their perception of the world, hinting at a greater force controlling the simulation.
